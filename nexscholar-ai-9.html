About NexScholar

NexScholar is a discovery platform for scholarships, admissions, grants, and other educational opportunities from around the world. Every listing links back to an official source, so you can move from browsing straight to applying with confidence.

Scholarships published on our partner blog appear here automatically, organised by country, degree level, and funding type, alongside our own curated listings — searchable in one place instead of scattered across dozens of sites.

Have a scholarship to suggest, or spotted something that looks off? Reach out — we're actively building this out and want it to stay accurate and useful.

How it works

  • Opportunities are published as posts on our content blog, tagged by country, degree, and funding type.
  • They sync into NexScholar automatically and become searchable, filterable listings.
  • Create a free account to bookmark opportunities and track ones you're applying to.
